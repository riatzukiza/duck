Crafting Recipes in Minecraft

Below are all the crafting recipes in Minecraft, including how to create various items and potions. For convenience, all recipes are grouped into categories.

Various tools are used for crafting in the game:

Crafting Table allows you to create most items using a 3x3 grid. Sword, Axe, Shovel, Pickaxe, Armor.

Furnace is used for smelting and cooking materials. Iron Ingots, Bricks, Glass, Cooked Food.

Smoker speeds up food cooking. Cooked Beef, Porkchop, Fish, Chicken.

Stonecutter simplifies stone block processing. Stone Bricks, Stairs, Slabs, Fence.

Campfire serves as a light source and can be used for cooking food. Cooking Fish, Meat, Lighting.


Beginners are recommended to start by chopping trees. Then press the E key to open the inventory and create Planks from the harvested wood. In the inventory, you can craft simple tools and items. You can make a Crafting Table from planks, which can be placed on the ground and allows you to create more complex items. To smelt and cook materials, a Furnace is required.
